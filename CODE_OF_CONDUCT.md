# WORLD 000 — Code of Conduct

## Our Purpose

WORLD 000 is a collaborative experiment where people create, compete, and discover possibilities inside a shared digital world.

We welcome participants from different countries, backgrounds, and experience levels.

## Community Standards

We encourage:

- Creative experimentation and constructive criticism.
- Respectful debate and technical disagreement.
- Collaboration between people with different skills.
- Fair competition within the world.
- Responsible reporting of security concerns.

We do not allow:

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Doxxing or unauthorized disclosure of private information.
- Attacks against real systems, servers, accounts, or infrastructure.
- Malicious code or attempts to compromise project infrastructure.
- Spam, impersonation, or deliberate disruption of community discussions.

## Competition and Security

Participants may challenge one another's creations within the boundaries of the world.

Competition inside the world does not authorize attacks against real people, systems, or services.

Security research must be conducted only with appropriate authorization.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate to the behavior and consistent with applicable platform policies.

## Reporting Concerns

For ordinary community concerns, contact the project maintainers through GitHub Discussions.

**Do not publicly disclose security vulnerabilities, credentials, private information, or exploit instructions.**

Until a dedicated private security reporting channel is configured, please do not submit sensitive vulnerability details through public Issues or Discussions.

The project will establish a private security reporting process before opening its services to public participation.

---

**Challenge ideas. Respect people. Protect the playground.**
