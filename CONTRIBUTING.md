# Contributing to WORLD 000

Thank you for your interest in WORLD 000.

WORLD 000 is an experimental, community-driven project. We welcome ideas, questions, technical proposals, and constructive criticism from people around the world.

## 1. Ways to Participate

You can participate by:

- Proposing ideas and experiments.
- Discussing technical architecture.
- Identifying design challenges.
- Reporting bugs and security concerns.
- Helping develop the first working prototype.

Start by joining our [community discussion](https://github.com/world000-lab/WORLD-000/discussions/1).

## 2. Creative Freedom

WORLD 000 does not prescribe civilizations, weapons, technology trees, strategies, or victory conditions.

Participants are encouraged to invent possibilities rather than follow a predetermined gameplay design.

The project maintainers retain responsibility for platform infrastructure, security, and repository governance.

## 3. Before Submitting Code

Please open a Discussion or Issue describing your proposal before beginning substantial development.

**Do not submit substantial code contributions until the project has published and confirmed its licensing and contribution terms.**

This protects both contributors and the project.

## 4. Intellectual Property

Contributors retain ownership of their original work unless they explicitly agree otherwise.

Submitting an idea, comment, or proposal does not automatically transfer intellectual property ownership to WORLD 000.

Code contributions will be governed by the applicable license and any separately agreed contribution terms.

The project name, branding, official infrastructure, and administrative decisions remain under the control of the project operator, subject to applicable law.

## 5. Security and Fair Competition

Competition belongs inside the game environment.

Do not attack real systems, accounts, services, or people.

Do not submit malicious code, credentials, private information, or unauthorized security exploits.

Report security vulnerabilities privately to the maintainers rather than publishing exploit details.

## 6. Review and Acceptance

Maintainers may review, request changes to, or decline contributions based on technical quality, security, compatibility, and project needs.

Discussion or submission does not guarantee acceptance.

## 7. Community Conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and open to disagreement.

Challenge ideas and strategies, not people.
