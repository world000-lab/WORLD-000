## 3. Before Submitting Code

WORLD 000 welcomes ideas, discussions, and technical proposals.

Participation is voluntary. Contributors are free to explore their own ideas without following a predetermined gameplay design.

**Until a formal software license and contribution agreement are published, please do not submit substantial code contributions or other substantial copyrighted materials.**

Contributors retain ownership of their original work unless they explicitly agree otherwise.

Any future use, modification, distribution, or commercialization of contributed code will require appropriate permissions under the applicable license or a separate agreement.

The project operator retains control of the WORLD 000 name, branding, official infrastructure, and platform governance, subject to applicable law.
