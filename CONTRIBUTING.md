# Contributing to WORLD 000

Thank you for your interest in WORLD 000.

WORLD 000 is an experimental, community-driven project where participants create possibilities inside a shared digital world.

**We create the world. You create its history.**

## 1. Ways to Participate

Participation is voluntary.

You may:

- Share ideas and experiments.
- Discuss technical architecture.
- Propose new possibilities.
- Identify design challenges.
- Report bugs and security concerns.
- Help improve the platform's technical foundation.

Start with our [community discussion](https://github.com/world000-lab/WORLD-000/discussions/1).

## 2. Creative Freedom

WORLD 000 does not prescribe civilizations, weapons, technology trees, strategies, or victory conditions.

Participants are free to explore and develop their own ideas.

The founder provides the playground, not a predetermined game.

Platform maintainers remain responsible for infrastructure, security, and repository governance.

## 3. Before Submitting Code

WORLD 000 welcomes ideas, discussions, and technical proposals.

**Until a formal software license and contribution agreement are published, please do not submit substantial code contributions or other substantial copyrighted materials.**

Contributors retain ownership of their original work unless they explicitly agree otherwise.

Any future use, modification, distribution, or commercialization of contributed code will require appropriate permissions under the applicable license or a separate agreement.

## 4. Intellectual Property

Submitting an idea, comment, or proposal does not automatically transfer intellectual property ownership to WORLD 000.

Code contributions will be governed by the applicable license and any separately agreed contribution terms.

The project operator retains control of the WORLD 000 name, branding, official infrastructure, and platform governance, subject to applicable law.

## 5. Security and Fair Competition

Competition belongs inside the world.

Do not attack real systems, accounts, services, or people.

Do not submit malicious code, credentials, private information, or unauthorized security exploits.

Security vulnerabilities must not be publicly disclosed through Issues or Discussions.

## 6. Review and Acceptance

Maintainers may review, request changes to, or decline contributions based on technical quality, security, compatibility, and project needs.

Discussion or submission does not guarantee acceptance.

## 7. Community Conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and open to disagreement.

Challenge ideas and strategies, not people.

---

**I built the playground. You make the game.**
